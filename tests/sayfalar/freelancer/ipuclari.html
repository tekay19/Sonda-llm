How to win your first jobs

  1. Specific title: clients search by specialty. "Python Developer | Web Scraping & Automation" beats "Developer".
  2. Overview: 150-300 words. Open with the client's problem, list 2-3 concrete results, end with a call to action.
  3. Rate: new freelancers with no reviews win more often at or slightly below the market rate. Python automation market median: $35-45/hr.
  4. Skills: add 8-15 relevant skills so you appear in more searches.
  5. Portfolio: profiles with at least 2 portfolio items get 3x more invites.
